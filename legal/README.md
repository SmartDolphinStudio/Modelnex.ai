# Legal And Third-Party Notice Index

This directory holds the legal material and third-party notices retained for the static ModelNex.AI frontend.

## Layout

- `modelnex/` - ModelNex.AI legal statements, compliance notices, user-facing terms, privacy policy, risk notices, and admin compliance documents.
- `third-party/sub2api/` - preserved upstream license, copyright, CLA, and original README notices.
- Local ignored folders contain migration history only. They are not part of the active source tree or deployment.

## Rules

- Do not delete third-party license, copyright, original README, CLA, or disclaimer material.
- Do not rewrite third-party legal text as if ModelNex authored it.
- If a third-party component is removed from active source, keep its legal backup here unless legal review explicitly says otherwise.
- Keep secrets out of legal documents and final reports.
